About Darul Uloom Sabeel Ul Rasool

Achagoza, Pulwama, Jammu & Kashmir

Institutional Overview

Founded with a vision to preserve and disseminate traditional Islamic education alongside moral discipline, Darul Uloom Sabeel Ul Rasool serves as a center of religious learning in the Achagoza area of District Pulwama, Jammu & Kashmir.

The institution imparts foundational and advanced education in Quranic Memorization (Hifz), Tajweed-o-Qiraat, Arabic morphology and syntax (Nahw & Sarf), Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh), and the Prophetic Hadith traditions (Dars-e-Nizami).

Core Objectives

  • Preservation and systematic memorization of the Holy Quran.
  • Instruction in Arabic grammar and classical Islamic literature.
  • Fostering social ethics, civic responsibility, and peace.
  • Preparing graduates capable of guiding their communities with moderation and wisdom.

Administrative Governance

The institution is administered through a collective consultative council (Majlis-e-Shura), composed of esteemed religious scholars and local community elders, ensuring financial transparency, academic rigor, and adherence to authentic scholastic methodologies.

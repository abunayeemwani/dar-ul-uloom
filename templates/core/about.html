About Darul Uloom Sabeel Ul Rasool

Achagoza, Pulwama, Jammu & Kashmir

Institutional Overview

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ، الصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِهِ الْأَمِينِ

وَمَا كَانَ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ لِيَنْفِرُوا كَافَّةً ۚ فَلَوْلَا نَفَرَ مِن كُلِّ فِرْقَةٍ مِّنْهُمْ طَائِفَةٌ لِّيَتَفَقَّهُوا فِي الدِّينِ وَلِيُنذِرُوا قَوْمَهُمْ إِذَا رَجَعُوا إِلَيْهِمْ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَحْذَرُونَ

"And it is not necessary for all the believers to go forth together. Why should not a group from every community go forth to gain understanding of the religion and warn their people when they return to them, so that they may become cautious?"

مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ

"Whoever follows a path in pursuit of knowledge, Allah will make the path to Paradise easy for him."

By the grace and mercy of Allah, in 2005, a meeting of Jamiat Ahl-e-Hadith, Halqa Keller, was held under the chairmanship of respected Muhammad Ashraf Wani Sahib. Its objective was to establish an institution in the area where people could acquire pure Tawheed (monotheism) and Prophetic knowledge.

Later, consultations were held with prominent religious scholars of the region, including Maulana Abdul Aziz Gorsi Al-Madani, Maulana Noor-ud-Din Noorani Al-Madani, Muhammad Afzal Sheikh, and other respected scholars.

After receiving their approval and encouragement, the proposal was presented to the respected President of Jamiat Ahl-e-Hadith Jammu and Kashmir, Maulana Showkat Ahmad Shah (May Allah have mercy on him).

The respected President appreciated this noble initiative and appointed Maulana Abdul Aziz Gorsi Al-Madani as the institution's Chairman on behalf of the Jamiat.

Subsequently, Maulana Noor-ud-Din Noorani Al-Madani named the institution Madrasa Sabeel-ur-Rasool.

In April 2006, the first batch of children was admitted. Respected Maulana Ghulam Rasool (May Allah have mercy on him) delivered the first lesson and made supplications for the institution's success.

Later, respected Muhammad Iqbal Al-Madani (May Allah preserve him) was appointed as a teacher at the madrasa. He subsequently changed its name to Darul Uloom Sabeel-ur-Rasool.

By the grace of Allah, the institution is progressing towards its objectives. Many Huffaz (those who have memorised the Holy Quran) and Islamic preachers have graduated from this institution and are engaged in spreading the message of pure Tawheed in different areas.

We pray to Allah Almighty to grant this institution continuous growth and progress. Ameen.

Vision

1. To teach Muslim youth to read the Holy Quran correctly with Tajweed and develop love, respect, and devotion towards the Quran.

2. To familiarise students with the teachings of the Quran and Sunnah, eliminate Shirk (polytheism) and Bid'ah (religious innovations), and spread the message of pure Tawheed.

3. To prepare Islamic preachers who can contribute effectively to Islam and Islamic Dawah and play a positive role in reforming society.

Core Objectives

  • Preservation and systematic memorization of the Holy Quran.
  • Instruction in Arabic grammar and classical Islamic literature.
  • Fostering social ethics, civic responsibility, and peace.
  • Preparing graduates capable of guiding their communities with moderation and wisdom.

Administrative Governance

The institution is administered through a collective consultative council (Majlis-e-Shura), composed of esteemed religious scholars and local community elders, ensuring financial transparency, academic rigor, and adherence to authentic scholastic methodologies.
